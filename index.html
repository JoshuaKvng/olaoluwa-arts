About the artist

My art, my expression

To me, art is much more than drawing or making beautiful images. It is my language, one of the ways I communicate what I sometimes struggle to put into words.

My drawings reflect my emotional state. When I am unhappy, my work carries sorrow, pain and introspection. When I am happy, that happiness finds its way into the composition.

I want my art to do more than please the eyes. I want it to provoke thought and preserve stories, so that people question something, or recognise a part of themselves or their history in it.
